# Field Record – rebuild notes

Rebuilt from https://journal-freestyle.grok.me/, which is a React/TanStack app built with Tailwind ("A private experiment log for meditation practice").

The screenshots of that app use a different theme. The default look is still paper: serif type, background `#f3efe6`, surface `#fbf8f2`, ink `#1c1914`, muted `#6e675c`, line `#e2dacb`, accent `#2f5d45`, 1rem rounded cards. Look swaps in Wood, Vine, or 90s Retro, a plain colour, or a custom skin.

## Structure
- `index.html`: the page shell. Home has the header ("Field journal" label, "Name this day" input with placeholder "New Day", date, Backup button, Look button), a Goals section, an Entries section with the category grid, the calendar card and the Days card. A category screen or a goal screen replaces that view while one is open. Backup and Look are only on the home header. A lightbox sits over the page for entry images.
- `styles.css`: palette as CSS variables. Every screen uses one width, `min(92vw, var(--content-max))`, with `--content-max` at 1100px: the home header, goals, entries, calendar, days list, category screen, goal screen, and the Look picker. Category cards are a bit taller. Goals are three columns at that width, two on a tablet, and one on a phone. Categories stay two columns, or one on a phone. Tap targets stay around 2.75rem.
- `app.js`: vanilla JS with no build step. Journal state is saved to localStorage under `field-record-v2`. Image bytes, including an uploaded theme background, live in IndexedDB (`field-record-images`, object store `blobs`), referenced by id, so a photo does not have to fit in localStorage. A running stopwatch is a separate key, `field-record-watch`. Forecast responses are a separate key, `field-record-weather`, so a weather lookup does not rewrite the journal.

### Record shape
`{look, skin, themes, borders, epoch, typing, lastBackupAt, backupSnoozeUntil, categories:[{id,name,type,tags,sectionNames,sectionTags,place,records}], entries:[{id,categoryId,date,title,note,tags,images,createdAt,timerStart,timerEnd,durationMinutes,sleepStart,sleepEnd,dreams,sections,condition,temperature}], goals:[{id,name,type,items,tags,days,entries}], dayTitles:{'YYYY-MM-DD': title}}`

- Missing `type` becomes `notes`. Missing `tags` or `images` become `[]`. Missing or unknown `look` becomes `paper`. A `theme:` id that is not in `themes` becomes `paper` too. Older records stay on the same key; extra fields are left in place.
- `look` is `paper`, `wood`, `vine`, `retro`, a plain colour (`red`, `blue`, `yellow`, `green`, `orange`, `brown`, `tide`, `sage`, `plum`), `custom`, or `theme:<id>`. A preset is applied with `data-look` and `skin` null. A custom skin sets `data-look="custom"` plus `data-border`, `data-font`, and inline colour variables. An older `earth` or `water` look becomes `custom`: the colours and font stay, the background becomes a plain colour, and the border becomes rounded. A saved theme that used an Earth or Water scene or frame is treated the same way. An uploaded background id is kept.
- `epoch` is `0` until Start fresh. A clear stores `Date.now()` in `epoch`. When two journals sync, the higher epoch replaces the other one entirely. Edits from the older epoch are dropped, including ones with a newer timestamp. Images that exist only on the older side are tombstoned so the server copy is deleted.
- `skin` is `{scene, uploadId, border, borderId, font, page, card, ink, accent, line}` or null. `border` can be `custom`, and then `borderId` picks one entry in `borders`. `themes` is a list of `{id, name, skin}`. `borders` is `[{id, sourceId, frameId, thickness, fit, orientation, tint, opacity, slice}]`. `thickness` is 8–60. `fit` is `tile` or `stretch`. `orientation` is `edge` or `tile`. `tint` is a hex colour or empty. `opacity` is 0.2–1. The original upload is `sourceId` (`bsrc-`) and the generated frame is `frameId` (`bframe-`). Both live in the image store.
- `typing` is `{enabled, voice, volume}`. It starts off. `voice` is `key`, `type`, or `pop`.
- `tags` on a category are the watched words. `tags` on an entry are the words chosen for that entry.
- `images` is a list of ids. The blob is a JPEG or WebP downscaled so the long edge is at most about 1600px. An uploaded background uses the same store, with an id prefixed `bg-`. A custom border keeps the original and the generated frame in that store too.
- Category `type` picks a form from the `TYPES` registry (`notes`, `timer`, `weather`, `list`, `todo`, `tracker`, `relationships`, `record`, `walk`). A new type is another entry in that registry: blank draft, read from an entry, write back, form fields, and card body. An old `sleep` category is stored as `timer`. `sleepStart` / `sleepEnd` are copied to `timerStart` / `timerEnd` when those are empty, and a save mirrors the timer times back onto the sleep fields.
- A weather category stores `place` once: `{name, admin1, country, latitude, longitude}` or null. A weather entry stores `condition` (`sunny`, `cloudy`, `rain`, `snow`, `storm`, `wind`, `fog`, or empty), `temperature` as a number in °C or null, a `title`, plus the usual note, tags, and images. An unknown condition becomes empty. A temperature that is not a finite number becomes null. Extra fields stay.
- A list category stores `lists` and `meals`. `lists` is `[{id,name,items:[{id,text,amount,checked}]}]`, kept on the category so it stays when the day changes. `amount` is a number or null. `checked` is true or false. `meals` is `{ 'YYYY-MM-DD': [{id,name,items:[{id,sourceId,text,amount}]}] }`. A meal item copies the list item's text and number. A missing `lists` becomes `[]` and a missing `meals` becomes `{}`. Extra fields stay.
- A To-Do category stores `todos` as `[{id,name,carry,items:[{id,text,added,done,doneAt}]}]`. `carry` is true or false and starts false. `added` and `done` are `YYYY-MM-DD`. `done` is empty until the item is completed, and `doneAt` is the completion time or `0`. A missing `todos` becomes `[]`. Extra fields stay.
- A tracker category stores `tracker` as `shifts` (Work shifts) or `flow` (Flow). An older `period` tracker becomes `flow`. Anything else becomes `shifts`. `shifts` is `{ 'YYYY-MM-DD': [{id,start,end,breakMinutes,label}] }`. `start` and `end` are `HH:MM`. `breakMinutes` is a number of unpaid minutes, or null. `label` is a string. A shift whose end is earlier than its start runs into the next day. Paid time is the span minus the break, and never below zero. A Flow tracker stores `flowOptions` as `[{id,name,color,shape,period}]` and `flow` as `{ 'YYYY-MM-DD': [{id,optionId,time}] }`. `color` is a hex colour. `shape` is `dot`, `square`, `diamond`, `triangle`, `heart`, `star`, `ring`, or `drop`. `period` is true when that option counts as a period. A missing `period` becomes true for a name like Light, Medium, or Heavy, and false for a name like Spotting or for any other name. `time` is `HH:MM` or empty. `showCycle` is true or false. `periodMarks` is `{ 'YYYY-MM-DD': true|false }`, with `periodMarkAt` holding the time of each choice. `true` forces that day to start a cycle. `false` means it is not a start. A missing `periodMarks` becomes `{}`. A missing `shifts` becomes `{}`. A missing `flow` becomes `{}` and a missing `flowOptions` becomes `[]`. Extra fields stay.
- `goals` is a list of goals that stay on the home screen no matter which day is selected. Missing `goals` becomes `[]`. The first type is `checks` (Daily check-boxes): `items` is `[{id,name}]`, `tags` are the watched words for that goal, `days` is `{ 'YYYY-MM-DD': {checks} }`, and `entries` is `[{id,date,title,note,tags,images,createdAt}]`. `checks` lists item ids marked done on that date. A new week has no checks until they are marked, and older dates stay in `days`. A day that still has a `note` or `tags` is copied into one entry for that date, then those fields are cleared. Unknown goal types are stored as `checks`. Extra fields on a goal are left in place.
- A Record category stores `records` as `[{id,title,note,tags,images,createdAt}]`. A record is not given a day. Missing `records` becomes `[]`. `createdAt` is the time it was first saved. Extra fields stay.
- A timer category has `sectionNames`, the names of its note sections in order, and `sectionTags`, the watched words for each section name. A new timer category starts with `['Notes']`. A category that was Sleep starts with `['Dreams','Notes']`. A timer entry stores `sections:[{id,name,title,text,tags}]`. `title` is optional and `tags` defaults to `[]` on older sections. Older timer and sleep entries are migrated: `note` becomes a section named Notes, and dream text becomes a section named Dreams (Dreams first). The Notes and Dreams section texts are also copied back onto `note` and `dreams`. A Notes-category entry keeps a single `note` and does not gain sections. A timer category does not keep entry-level tags: on load its `tags` become `[]`, and each of its entries has `tags` set to `[]`. Section tags stay. A Notes category keeps its tags.

### Behaviour
- Earlier / Later move the selected day by ±1 and Today jumps back to today. The chevrons change the month shown.
- Clicking the month/year heading opens a picker: Jan–Dec, plus year arrows and a year field. Choosing a month jumps the calendar there. Calendar days with content are outlined, the selected day is filled with the accent colour, and today is underlined.
- Home starts with Goals, then Entries. `+ New goal` asks for a name and a type. A Daily check-boxes card is a compact week, Sunday to Saturday, for the week that contains the selected day. Each item is one row: the name, then that week’s checks beside it. Today’s column is highlighted. Clicking a square toggles that item on that date. Under the grid, the card shows that day’s saved entry titles and tags. The goal title opens a screen laid out like a Notes category: Back and the date, the goal name as the heading (click it to rename), then one framed card. Inside the card, the same week sits above a New item field with Add item and Edit. Add item, from the button or Enter, clears that field and leaves the cursor there. Edit turns into Done, and each name can be renamed in place, moved up or down, or removed with ×. The first item’s up arrow and the last item’s down arrow are disabled. The order is saved and shown on the home card. A check stays with its item. Below that, the day’s log matches Notes: Title, a tall Note, Tags you are watching, images, and Save entry. Saving stores that log and clears the form for a new entry, including the title, note, chosen tags, and images. Cancel does the same. Notes, Timer, and Weather already clear their forms the same way after Save entry. Saved entries for the selected day are cards under the form, and Edit or Delete loads or removes one. Delete goal is a small link at the bottom of the card and asks before it removes the goal. Add tag stores the word on the goal and attaches it to the entry being written. Goals use the current look’s card frame, including on the goal screen.
- A new category asks for a name and a type: Notes, Timer, Weather, List, To-Do, Tracker, Relationships, Record, or Walk. Tracker then asks for Work shifts or Flow. Tracker cards sit at the top of the Entries grid, then List cards, then To-Do cards, then the other categories. The stored order inside each group is left as it was. There is no starter category; a new journal begins empty, the same way as before.
- A Weather category asks for a city once, searched through the Open-Meteo geocoding API (no key), or Use my location. The home card and the category screen show current conditions when they are known, and the selected day's high, low, and precipitation. Today and the next six days come from the forecast API. Earlier days come from the archive API, and a recent day that the archive has not filled in yet falls back to the forecast's past days. Farther ahead than seven days, the screen says the forecast only goes that far. Results are cached in `field-record-weather` (about 30 minutes for the forecast, about 12 hours for an archive day). If a refresh fails, the cached summary stays up with "Showing saved weather." If there is nothing cached, it says the weather is unavailable, and the log can still be saved. The log is a condition (Sunny, Cloudy, Rain, Snow, Storm, Wind, Fog), a temperature prefilled from the forecast when the field is still empty, How it felt (the same chips, Add tag, and Edit, and a new word attaches to the entry), a Title, a Notes box of the same tall size, and images. Entry cards, the home card, and the expanded day review show the title when it is not just the condition name, then the condition icon, the temperature, and the tags.
- A Tracker category uses the same Back, date, and heading as Notes, then a compact month calendar with Earlier, Today, and Later. Work shifts: add a start, an end, an optional unpaid break in minutes, and an optional label. A day can hold more than one shift. Edit and × change or remove one. Days with shifts show a small mark and the paid hours, such as `7.5h`. Each week in the month, Sunday to Saturday, has a total beside the calendar on a wide screen and under it on a narrow one, and the month has a total. Flow: the options are named by the user, the same way as tags. Add option clears the field and leaves the cursor there. Edit renames an option, removes it, or moves it up and down; the end arrows are disabled. Each option has a colour from a short soft palette or a custom colour, and a shape: dot, square, diamond, triangle, heart, star, ring, or drop, at the same small size as the old dot. Edit also shows a small Counts as period toggle. Spotting starts off. Light, Medium, and Heavy start on. A newly added option starts off until it is turned on. The options sit in a grid, four columns, or two on a narrow phone. Edit uses one column so the palette, shapes, and toggle fit. Tapping an option logs it on the selected day. The same option can be logged more than once, shown as ×2. A log does not ask for a time. Older logs may still store `time`, and that value stays in the journal and in sync. × removes one log. The calendar shows up to three of that day's icons, then `+n`. Below the calendar, the day's log matches Notes: Title, a tall Note, tags, images, and Save entry, and the form clears after a save. A saved entry shows that day's icons and labels. Show cycle stats is off on a new Flow tracker. When it is on, the calendar card shows the cycle day, the last start, the average cycle length, the average length of a logged run, and a predicted next start once two ordinary cycles are known. A cycle starts on a day whose logged option counts as a period, after a gap. A spotting-only day stays on the calendar and does not start a cycle or add to the length. Spotting days between counted days stay in the same period and still do not add a day. If no counted day has started, the card says `Cycle day —`. The length of a run is the number of counted days in it. Period started forces the selected day to begin a cycle. Not a start suppresses an automatic one. Those choices sync and are part of a backup. Cycles shorter than 15 days, longer than 45 days, or longer than about 1.6 times the median are left out of the average and the prediction. When one is left out, a small line says `(1 skipped cycle ignored)`. If every cycle would be left out, the plain average is used and that line stays hidden. An older Period tracker becomes Flow with Spotting, Light, Medium, and Heavy, dot-shaped, in the old pale-to-deep shades, the same logged days, and cycle stats on. The home card for Work shifts shows that day's shifts and the week's hours. The Flow card shows that day's icons and labels, and the cycle day when stats are on and a counted day has started. The expanded day review shows that day's shifts, or that day's flow logs and a Period started or Not a start mark, and the cycle day when one of those is present. A later cycle day with no log and no mark is left out. Tracker cards sit at the top of the Entries grid, then List cards, then To-Do cards, then the other categories. The stored order inside each group is left as it was. A backup file is the whole journal, so shifts, flow options, logs, and period marks travel with export and import.
- A To-Do category uses the same Back, date, and heading as Notes. Lists sit in a stack, like meals: Add list names one, such as Errands or Work, and each list has its own items. A check is a circle. Tapping it marks the item done for the selected day, fades it out of the active list, and moves it into a Completed section under that list, with the completion date and time in small type. Tapping the check again restores it. Completed can be collapsed. Carry over to next day starts off. While it is off, the list is only for the day an item was added: the next day is empty, and the list name stays so new items can be added. While it is on, unfinished items appear on later days, including today after a gap, until they are completed. A completed item stays on the day it was completed and does not appear on a later day. The home card shows that day's open and done counts. The expanded day review and a PDF show that day's lists: items still open that day, and items completed that day. Edit renames, reorders, or deletes lists and open items. A backup is the whole journal, so to-dos travel with export and import. Each list and each item syncs on its own. Start fresh clears them with the rest of the journal data and keeps the category unless categories are reset too.
- A List category screen uses the same Back, date, and heading as Notes. Lists sit in a grid, two columns from a tablet width and one column on a phone. Each list is a card: the name, then rows with a checkbox, the item text, and an optional number. New item, or Enter, adds a row, clears the field, and leaves the cursor there. Edit on a list renames, removes, or moves its items; the first item's up arrow and the last item's down arrow are disabled. Clear checked turns the checks off and leaves the items. Edit beside New list renames or deletes a list the same way as a tag. Lists are not tied to a day, and they do not show a total. Under the grid, Meals is for the selected day. Add meal, or Enter, adds a named meal such as Breakfast. Edit renames, deletes, or reorders meals, with the end arrows disabled. Add from lists opens every list item; choosing several and Add selected copies them into that meal with their numbers. × removes a copied line. Each meal shows Total, and the day shows Day total across meals. Below that, the day's log matches Notes: Title, a tall Note, tags, images, and Save entry, and the form clears after a save. The home card lists each list name with its item count, that day's meals with their totals and the day total, then that day's entry titles and tags. The expanded day review lists that category's notes and entries first, then each meal as its own framed card: the meal name, each item with its number, and the meal total, then the day total. A backup file is the whole journal, so lists and meals travel with export and import.
- Opening a category or a goal scrolls to the top. Back returns to the home scroll position. A sync refresh does not rebuild the screen while a text field is focused or still holds text that has not been copied into the draft. When the field blurs, the refresh runs and keeps that text, the caret, and the scroll position.
- A category card lists that day's entries. A notes entry shows its title, then tags as `Tag · Tag` in the accent colour. A timer entry shows its title, duration, and each section that has a title or tags (`Dreams: Flying over the ocean`, then that section's tags). Opening the card shows Back (top-left) and the selected date (top-right). There is no Look button on that screen. That day's entries sit below the form as cards. Clicking a card, or Edit, loads it into the form; Delete removes it and its image blobs.
- Notes form: Title, Note, watched tags, Add images, Save entry. The note box starts about 24rem tall, or 60% of the screen when that is shorter. Tab from the previous field still moves into the note. Once the note is focused, Tab indents and Shift+Tab outdents the current line. Escape, then Tab, moves focus out once. Ctrl or Cmd with B, I, or U toggles bold, italic, or underline on the selection. Enter after a line that starts with `1. ` (any number) or `- ` or `* ` continues the list, keeping the indent. Enter on a line that is only the marker removes it. The same keys work in every notes box: timer sections, weather, walks, records, relationship notes, and goal notes. Formatting is stored in the note string and shown in the editor, the day review, and a PDF. A note with no formatting still shows as plain text, including its line breaks. Markup is not treated as HTML.
- Timer form: Title, a stopwatch (Start, then Pause or Resume, and Stop & save), or a start and end time, or hours and minutes when the times are empty. Then named note sections, Add images, Save entry. There is no entry-level Tags block. `+ Add note section` adds a section with a name (placeholder "Section name"), an optional Title, a textarea about 24rem tall (capped at 60% of the screen on a short phone) that can be resized, and that section's own tags under the textarea. Up, Down, and Remove reorder or delete sections. The category keeps the section names, and the watched words for each section name, so the next entry starts with the same sections, empty, and the same chips. Adding a word stores it for that section name and attaches it to the section being written. Chips toggle, and Edit renames or deletes a watched word for that section name across the category. Renaming a section moves its watched list onto the new name when no other section still uses the old one, and the chips update as the name is typed. Stop & save writes the clock's start and end (it keeps running across reload and a background tab because it stores a start timestamp). If the end clock is earlier than the start clock, one midnight is assumed, so 11:15 PM to 7:00 AM is `7h 45m`. Cards, including the expanded day review, show the title, duration, start–end, and each section that has a title, tags, or text. The section name is a small heading, its title sits under that when one was written, and the section's tags sit under the title.
- Look opens a picker on the home screen. The choice covers the home screen, category screen, calendar, days list, and the picker. Each built-in look sets the page background, card fill, border, type, and button colours:
  - Paper: the default rounded paper cards, no picture frame.
  - Wood: warm grain (`assets/bg-wood.webp`), parchment cards, serif (Libre Baskerville). Frame is `assets/border-wood.webp` as a 9-slice `border-image`.
  - Vine: leafy ground (`assets/bg-vine.webp`), pale cards, Fraunces. Frame is `assets/border-vine.svg`, vines and leaves.
  - 90s Retro: pixel clouds (`assets/bg-retro.webp`) plus a faint pixel grid, chunky lime cards with a dark header bar and a bevel, Pixelify Sans. The frame is CSS, not a border image.
  - Plain colours, in their own group: Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Orange, and Brown, plus Orange & Blue, Sage & Terracotta, and Plum & Mustard. Each is a soft palette with a rounded edge and a small swatch. Orange & Blue and Plum & Mustard use a dark page with a light chip behind the title. A plain colour can still take a custom border, a font, or a saved theme.
- Customize, in the same picker, recolours the current look, including Paper. Background can be any built-in scene, a plain colour, or an uploaded image. Border can be Rounded, Square, Double line, Dashed, or Thick / shadowed, and those take the border colour, or a Wood or Vine frame. Upload border image takes one picture and builds a 9-slice frame: the picture runs along the top, rotated for the other three edges, with a square from each end turned into the corners. CSS `border-image` draws it on every card, including the stacked frames in a day review. Thickness is 8–60px. Tile repeats the edge (`round`); Stretch fills each edge once. Orientation is either "Runs along the edge" for a strip, or "Image is a tile" for a square pattern. Tint and opacity are baked into the frame. A row of thumbnails keeps several borders, and each can be deleted. The preview card in the panel uses the same frame. Save theme stores a named copy that points at the chosen border. Deleting the theme you are using returns to Paper. Deleting a border drops it from any skin or saved theme that used it, and removes its two images. An Earth or Water journal keeps its colours, font, and any uploaded background, and uses Paper's plain background and rounded border.
- Backup, beside Look, opens a small panel. Export backup downloads `field-record-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`. The file is format version 1 and holds the whole `field-record-v2` record (entries, goals, lists, meals, to-dos, shifts, flow, day names, looks, saved themes, and custom borders) plus every IndexedDB image, including an uploaded background and both images of a custom border, as base64. A backup with a `borders` field that is not a list is rejected. An older backup with no `borders` still imports. The weather cache and the running stopwatch are left out. Import backup reads a file, shows how many days, entries, and images it holds and the export date, then asks you to confirm with "This replaces everything on this device" before it replaces the journal and the image store. A file that is not format 1, or that fails to parse, leaves the current journal and images as they were. `lastBackupAt` is the time of the last successful export. Until there is one, or when it is more than seven days old, the home screen shows "It's been a while since your last backup", with Export now and Later. Later sets `backupSnoozeUntil` three days ahead and hides the note until then. The panel shows "Last backup: date".
- Export PDF, in Backup and on the Days card, asks for a range: This week, Last week, This month (Sunday to Saturday for the weeks), or From and To. It prints the day reviews in order, oldest first, and skips a day with nothing to show. The print is on white paper, with the day name, the categories that have something that day, goals, and a drawn walk route when a walk has one. Bold, italic, underline, and indents are included. Save as PDF from the browser's print dialog. It does not need a network.
- Start fresh, in Backup, offers Download a backup first and Export PDF, then asks for the word CLEAR. By default it clears entries, day names, goals, lists, meals, to-dos, shifts, flow logs, records, relationships, walks, and images, and keeps the theme, fonts, borders, saved themes, the categories and their flow options, and the sync link. Also reset categories and settings clears those too, and still keeps the sync link. The panel then says "Journal cleared". The clear is an `epoch`. Another device that syncs takes that journal and drops its older copy, so the old entries do not merge back. Photos that belonged to the old copy are removed from the server.
- Typing sounds, in that same panel, are off until chosen: Keyboard, Typewriter, or Bubble, plus a volume slider. They are made with the Web Audio API (no audio files) and play while typing in a text field or textarea. The choice is saved on the record.
- Days rows use the look's frame. Expanding a day keeps that frame on the day and on each entry card, so the image frames stay stacked. Clicking a day expands it (click again to collapse) and lists only the categories that have something that day: notes or other entries, timer sessions, a weather log, list notes or meals, to-do items still open that day and items completed that day, shifts, flow logs or a period mark, relationship notes, walks, or records created that day. An empty category is left out, with no heading and no "Nothing written." After those, a Goals heading lists each goal that has something on that day. When no goal has anything, the Goals heading is left out. Each of those goals is one framed card, the same stacked frame as the other entries. The goal name is the heading inside the card. Daily check-boxes then show `2 of 4` and a small check beside each item that was marked. A goal note saved that day sits in the same card, with its title, tags, and note. A goal with nothing that day is left out, and these cards do not appear under the Entries grid. A day with only goal checks or goal notes still appears in the list. A day with nothing at all is left out, unless it has a name, and then the row shows that name. The collapsed row counts the same pieces the open review shows, entries and goals apart, such as `2 entries · 3 goals` or `3 goals`. A named day with nothing else has no count. Open day selects that day for the journal.
- Tags: "New word, kept in this category", Add tag, and Edit. Add tag stores the word on the category and attaches it to the entry being written. The same Add tag field, including each timer section and a goal’s tags, clears and keeps the cursor after the button or Enter. A chip click toggles that word. Edit turns the button into Done, shows an × on each chip, and a click on the chip text renames it inline. Renaming or deleting a watched tag updates entries in that category. Entry tags render as `Tag · Tag` in the accent colour.
- Add images accepts several `image/*` files. Thumbnails sit in the form with a remove ×, and on the card as a grid. A thumbnail opens a larger lightbox. If the browser cannot read or store a photo, the form says so and the rest of the entry is left as it was.

## Sync
Devices share one journal through a sync code. The code is four groups of five characters from `23456789ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ`. It is stored on this device in `field-record-sync`. PBKDF2-SHA256, 200,000 iterations, salt `field-record-sync-v1`, derives 64 bytes. The first 32 bytes, as hex, are the vault id. The last 32 bytes are the AES-GCM key. The journal, without typing sounds or backup reminders, is encrypted with a random 12-byte IV and stored as base64 of the IV plus ciphertext. Each image is encrypted the same way. Its id on the server is the SHA-256 hex of the local image id, and it is downloaded when a screen asks for it.

The server is Supabase project `https://nazegfvpkpypjvwmwwpf.supabase.co`. The app calls `POST /rest/v1/rpc/<fn>` with the publishable key. `fr_get_vault` reads `{data, version, updated_at}`. `fr_put_vault` writes with `p_base_version` and returns the new version, or `-1` when another device wrote first. On `-1` the app reads again, merges, and retries. `fr_list_images`, `fr_get_image`, `fr_put_image`, and `fr_delete_image` move the encrypted photos. The vault id is 64 hex characters. The vault payload stays under 5MB and each image under 15MB.

Records merge by id, including a custom border, when both devices are on the same `epoch`. The newer `updatedAt` wins for that record. A higher `epoch` wins the whole journal instead, and the older device's records are not merged back. A deleted border is a tombstone, and its original and frame are removed with the other images. The frame and the original are encrypted like every other image. Order uses its own timestamp, such as `listOrderAt` or `flowOptionOrderAt`. A deletion is a tombstone in `gone`, keyed by kind and id, so it is not undone by an older copy. Linking a device that already has a journal merges the two and says so. Typing sounds stay on the device. The app syncs on load, when the window is focused or shown, about three seconds after a save, about once a minute while it is open, and when the browser comes back online. Offline edits stay in the local journal and go up on the next successful sync. The header Code button shows the grouped code, Copy, and the status: `Synced 2 min ago`, `Syncing…`, `Offline, will sync later`, or the error. The full controls, including Unlink this device, sit at the bottom of Backup.

## Offline
`manifest.webmanifest` names the app Field Record, with theme `#2f5d45`, background `#f3efe6`, and 192 and 512 icons. `apple-touch-icon.png` and the Apple web-app meta tags cover Add to Home Screen. `sw.js` precaches the page, `styles.css?v=26`, `app.js?v=26`, Leaflet (`vendor/leaflet/leaflet.css`, `leaflet.js`, and the marker images), the manifest, the icons, and the theme images. Its cache is `field-record-v26`, bumped with the `?v=` query. The shell is cache-first. OpenStreetMap tiles (`tile.openstreetmap.org`) are cached the first time they load, and a failed tile request does not break the worker. Place search (`nominatim.openstreetmap.org`) tries the network first and falls back to a cached response. Google Fonts CSS and font files are cached the first time they load. Typing sounds are generated in the browser, so there are no sound files to store. When a new service worker is waiting, the page shows `Update ready, tap to refresh`. Weather that was already saved still shows offline, with a small `offline` note. A cold load after the first visit does not need a network.

## Record
A Record category uses the same Title, tall Note, tags, and images form as Notes. Add tag stores the word and attaches it to the record being written. Save record stores it on the category, not on the selected day, and clears the form. The list under the form is every saved record, newest first. Each row shows the title, tags, and a small created date. Clicking a row opens the note and images; Edit and Delete are on that open row. Clicking it again closes it. Once there are more than eight records, a Filter field matches a tag, the title, or the note. The home card shows the record count and the latest few titles on every selected day. The expanded day review shows only the records created on that day, with a small `saved` label. Records travel with backup and sync. A deleted record is a tombstone, and the newer copy wins if both devices edited it. Tags from both copies are kept.

## Relationships
A Relationships category stores `groups` as `[{id,name}]` and `people` as `[{id,groupId,name,photoId,tags,noteTags,sections,notes}]`. The screen uses the same Back, date, and heading as Notes. Add group sits in one framed card. Each group is its own framed card: the group name, the people count, the people grid, and the Name field with Add person. People are small cards in two columns, or one column on a phone, with a name, a photo or an initial, and their tags. Inside a framed group those person cards use a thin edge so the frames do not stack. A profile is the same framed card: info sections the user names, such as Birthday or How we met, each with its own text, plus person tags and an optional photo. Groups are added, renamed, removed, and reordered from Edit, like tags. A person can move to another group. Notes under the profile use a framed form: a title, a tall note, tags, and images, saved with the selected date, newest first. The form clears after Save note, and each saved note is its own framed card. Empty notes stay inside a card too. The home card and the day review show that day's notes as `Coffee with Sam · Friends`. People stay when the day changes. If a section named Birthday parses as a date, and that date is within 14 days, the card says `Birthday in 3 days`, `Birthday tomorrow`, or `Birthday today`. Photos and note images use the same image store as entries, so they are in a backup and in sync.

## Walk
A Walk category stores `walks` as `[{id,date,title,note,tags,images,createdAt,updatedAt,points,distanceM,durationMin}]` and `walkUnit` as `km` or `mi`. `points` is `[{lat,lng}]`. Distance is the length of that line. The last map centre is kept on this device in `field-record-walk-view` and is not synced. The map is Leaflet with OpenStreetMap tiles and the OpenStreetMap copyright link. Search a place recentres the map. The search waits about a second, asks for at most five matches, and cancels the previous request. Use my location recentres on the device when the browser allows it. Tap the map to add points. Undo last point, Clear, and Finish change the line without reloading the map. Finish with fewer than two points does nothing. Duration is optional minutes, and a pace shows when there is a distance and a duration. Save walk stores the title, note, tags, images, route, distance, and duration on the selected day. Saved walks sit below the form with a small map, title, tags, and distance. Tap one to edit the route. The home card lists today's walks and distance, plus the week's distance. The expanded day review shows each walk the same way, with its own small map. If the tiles cannot load, the map says so and the line can still be drawn. Walks travel with backup and sync. A deleted walk is a tombstone, and the newer copy wins.
